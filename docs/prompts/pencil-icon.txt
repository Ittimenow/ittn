Create ONE standalone icon for the IT TIME NOW website.

TARGET SUBJECT: One simple recognizable PENCIL, diagonal from lower left sharpened tip to upper right blunt end. Two chunky crooked parallel sides, triangular tip, one short end cap. Hollow body, no letters, no extra marks.
BLOCK CONTEXT: Interactive drawing tool beside handwritten ИТ in website hero.
WHAT THE ICON MUST COMMUNICATE: Pick up this pencil to draw on the page.

The attached approved bar-chart image is a STYLE AND PEN-WEIGHT reference only.
Do not copy its chart subject. Match its chunky pencil weight at the same overall
drawing size. Do not make each new icon progressively thicker than this reference.

Draw a simple, immediately recognizable silhouette with only essential details.
It must remain readable at 16–24 px for interface controls and at 48–64 px in cards.
Leave clear gaps between neighboring strokes and keep hollow shapes open.

Use rough, expressive, hand-drawn electric ultramarine blue wax-pencil line art.
ONE pigment only: #323BFF. Genuine transparent alpha background and hollow areas.
Preserve natural proportions on a square canvas. Tight framing: the longest side
of the drawing should occupy about 92% of the canvas, with about 4% transparent
margin at each end. The final project asset is 512×512 px, with the longest drawing
dimension about 472 px and minimum margins about 20 px.

CRUCIAL: use the SAME average chunky pencil weight as the approved reference and
the existing icon family, while varying pressure conspicuously WITHIN each stroke.
Dense broad bulges alternate naturally with substantially narrower dry portions,
roughly 2-to-3-fold local width variation. Heavy pressure deposits dense blue;
relaxed pressure creates scruffy broken grain and narrow stretches. Inner and
outer details should look as though they were drawn with the same pencil.

The trajectories themselves visibly bow, wobble, lean and bend. Corners, joins and
endings are clumsy and irregular. Vary the rhythm of each stroke; do not use a
repetitive sine-wave effect. Preserve uneven spacing, asymmetry, slight overshoots,
rough edges and visible pressure fluctuations. The result should look genuinely
hand-drawn, naive, spontaneous, friendly, scruffy and a little punk/DIY.

Do not merely add speckles to smooth geometric paths. Do not straighten, smooth,
regularize or turn the artwork into clean vector outlines. Grain and pressure must
be represented by alpha coverage and irregular edges, not a second color, glow,
gradient or shading.

Only the icon. No text, numbers, labels, decorative debris, frame, tile, paper,
background scene or extra elements. No white background or drawn checkerboard.

Negative prompt: photorealistic, realistic shading, 3D, gradients, glossy effects,
clean corporate vector art, geometric perfection, smooth bezier curves, perfectly
uniform stroke width, typography font, polished logo design, multiple colors,
shadows, detailed background, white background, black outlines, neat symmetric
composition, thin formal line art.
